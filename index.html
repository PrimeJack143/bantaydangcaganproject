Community Weather Warning System

📍 Dangcagan, Bukidnon ☀️ Solar Powered 📡 Works Offline 🕐 24/7

What is this?

A simple DIY system that watches the weather and warns our community before a storm, heavy rain, or strong wind hits.

It sends alerts through SMS, Facebook, sirens, and LoRa even without internet. Powered by the sun, so it keeps working during brownouts.
